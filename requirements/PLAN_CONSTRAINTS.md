# Working constraints carried forward from the conversation
This is a planning synthesis, not an excerpt of the audited DOCX.

The research unit is a bounded compound inference policy; a realized path is a stochastic trace through it. An initial option pool may include a tightly scoped calculator, a direct cheap model, a direct strong model with a short output cap, a bounded cheap/check/repair policy and a fixed-corpus retrieval policy. Tool applicability must be checked; deterministic computation does not guarantee that natural language was interpreted correctly.

Python is the first authoritative implementation. Java and TypeScript begin as clients/adapters; independently scoring snapshots comes only after exact semantics and cross-language fixtures exist. Keep Iceberg policy learning, compound execution, and provider transport separate. A native Java or TypeScript SDK is not a reason to duplicate training.

Separate estimated-cost allocation from upper-bound admission. A strict ceiling depends on enforceable operation limits, complete billing coverage, atomic authorization and reconciliation. Provider cancellation or timeout does not prove that no charge is due. Persistent ledger choice must match deployment guarantees; plain JSON files without a single-writer/locking/durability protocol do not establish concurrent budget safety.

Use real traces for conditional workflows. Include adaptation cost, served coverage, deferrals, and quality over the original workload. Label deterministic synthetic worlds, fixed outcome matrices, repeated generations, and human studies separately. Never infer general superiority from one successful sample.

For WISERouter comparisons, preserve paper-based expected-cost behavior as a reference. Separately name our reproduction, our common-guard variant, and our equal-option WR-Path adaptation. Do not transplant the paper's theoretical guarantees to those changes.

A source-linked implementation study and minimal baselines come before the Iceberg-specific acquisition strategy. The main question is whether that strategy improves net allocation enough to repay the information it buys. Negative or inconclusive results should simplify the architecture, not produce invented performance claims.
